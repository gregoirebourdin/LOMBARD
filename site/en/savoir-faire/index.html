Your chalet, on your plot

From 60 to 500 m², in solid timber post-and-beam: we draw, cut and raise everything ourselves.

A chalet in Puy-Saint-Vincent

Everything in-house

  1. Design officeAround ten people: design, costing, 3D, planning applications and site management.
  2. WorkshopThree to four technicians and carpenters turn 3D drawings into ready-to-assemble parts, in 1,300 m² and with the K2.
  3. On siteTwo teams of carpenter-fitters and roofers.

Post-and-beam, in solid timber

Since 2005 our chalets stand on a solid timber post-and-beam structure: long spans, large openings and a frame that stays visible.

Four woods for the interior

  • Larch
  • Thermo-treated spruce
  • Reclaimed wood
  • Thermo-treated larch

Bespoke interior fittings, made in our joinery workshop since 2016.

With a partner architect

We work with architect Alain Gallinet, for instance on the Freyssinet chalet in 2018 and on the Étoile des Glaciers.
