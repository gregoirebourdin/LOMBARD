A family, a team of 40

Lombard & Vasina has built chalets in the Grand Briançonnais since 1979, and nowhere else.

Our offices in Saint-Martin-de-Queyrières

A company ahead

  1. 1979Maurice Lombard and Jean-Claude Vasina join forces to build turnkey chalets, known for red cedar and over-rafter roof insulation.
  2. 1989A joinery workshop, fitted with state-of-the-art machines.
  3. 2000Computer-aided design (CadWork), then a K2 CNC machine to cut timber: a first in the Hautes-Alpes.
  4. 2005The move to post-and-beam construction.
  5. 2016A CNC machine for joinery, and bespoke interior fittings.
  6. 2020A 1,300 m² framing workshop, a new K2 and a panel production line.
  7. 2021Marielle, a civil engineer, and Mathieu take over the company. Maurice stays on to pass on his know-how.

Three trades, one place

Nearly 40 people, all in-house: around ten in the design office, three to four technicians and carpenters in the workshop, two teams of carpenter-fitters and roofers on site.

Marielle, a civil engineer, and Mathieu joined their father about ten years ago and took over the company in 2021. Maurice stays on to pass on his know-how.
