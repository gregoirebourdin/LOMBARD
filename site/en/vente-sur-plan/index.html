Buying off-plan, with confidence

Buying off-plan (VEFA, the French “sale before completion” scheme) gives you the security of a regulated purchase and the freedom of a bespoke project: materials, layouts, finishes.

Our chalets are drawn, cut and raised by our own teams, in our workshops in Saint-Martin-de-Queyrières.

Plans, 3D views and tours are sent on request. Prices are not published: we share them with you directly.
