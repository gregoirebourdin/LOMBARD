Tour in 360°

Ten chalets built by our teams, to explore room by room. Each was designed for the people who live in it.

  • Chalet Dibona Delivered in 2023. About 227 m², spa area.
  • Chalet Écrin des Vignes Spa, sauna and games room.
  • Chalet Ski Resort 134 m², 5 bedrooms, cathedral living room.
  • Demi-chalet Through living room at the foot of the slopes, two homes.
  • Chalet des Serres 190 m², 4 bedrooms and a bunk room, sauna.
  • Chalet Reine All in thermo-treated larch.
  • Chalet Taou-Manou Light, warm interiors.
  • Chalet contemporain Designed with architect Alain Gallinet.
  • Chalet Aigle Sleeps up to 14, view over the Serre Chevalier valley.
  • Chalet Le Lorgnon 5 bedrooms, sleeps up to 12.

Tours are hosted by Klapty and only load when opened. For a chalet for sale, the tour is sent on request.
